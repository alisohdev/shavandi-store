# Asset credits

Eight primary product photos come from the real product-detail pages at [Shavandi Store](https://shavandistore.ir/) and [Shavandi Sport](https://shavandisport.ir/). Names, current prices, and listed attributes were audited from fresh HTML on **2026-11-02**. The user explicitly confirmed reuse rights for these photos on that date. The complete [asset manifest](asset-manifest.json) records each product page, original photo URL, verified facts, demo fields, source dimensions, and local derivative dimensions. Raw downloaded JPEGs and source HTML stay outside the repository.

Local files under `public/images/shavandi/` are WebP derivatives at 320 pixels, 480 pixels where the original permits, and original width. Originals range from 434 to 600 pixels wide; nothing is enlarged or cropped. Branding and visible image content are retained. Product cards, hero, collection pages, gallery, checkout, and editor use responsive local sources and preserve full garments with `object-fit: contain`.

These eight records have source prices. Availability, cart, shipping, and checkout are still simulated. The shark height range, listed blanket colors, sports sizes, and France player-version title are the verified attributes; no official-license, season, fan-version, material, or care claim is inferred. Blanket sizes other than the shark height range are explicitly demo.

The photographs below are retained as **temporary Unsplash references** for the original eight sample products and women's category. They do not represent real Shavandi inventory. All associated product details and prices are sample content, with visible temporary labels. The men's category uses `mens-placeholder.svg`, an original illustration created for this preview, visibly marked «تصویر موقت». It has no fictional product inventory.

The [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license) describes the source's reuse terms. Original image URLs are retained below for provenance. A [weserv image proxy](https://images.weserv.nl/) was used at development time because the image CDN could not be reached directly from this machine. The website makes no requests to either service at runtime.
